Cullman is in North Alabama: 45 miles south of Huntsville, 45 miles north of Birmingham, and about 156 miles from Atlanta.

From I-65

Take exit 308 and head east on Highway 278 through Cullman. St. Bernard is on the right after you cross a long bridge. Follow the signs to Ave Maria Grotto.

Staying overnight

Find hotels through the Cullman Chamber of Commerce. Festival guests also get reduced admission to the Ave Maria Grotto all weekend.

Bloomin' Festival

Held each spring by St. Bernard Abbey and St. Bernard Preparatory School, 1600 St. Bernard Drive SE, Cullman, Alabama. Proceeds support St. Bernard Prep. Neighbors of the Ave Maria Grotto.
