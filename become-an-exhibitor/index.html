Before you apply

Festival rules

Bloomin' Festival is held outdoors, rain or shine. All fees are non-refundable.

  • Hours and setup

    Open 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. each day. Setup can begin the Friday before. Booths must be ready by 8 a.m. on Saturday and Sunday.

  • Artwork

    Handmade work only. No imports, commercial items or kits.

  • Displays

    Bring your own racks, tables, chairs and covers. Tables must be covered uniformly to the ground. The coordinator may remove any item that is in bad taste or was not approved.

  • Booth space

    Each booth is about 12′ × 12′. If your display won't fit, buy two spaces.

  • Parking

    No motor homes, travel trailers or vehicles larger than a standard van. Cars and small trailers may park in the school lot. Local campgrounds can host weekend vendors.

  • Conduct

    No pets. Alcohol and drugs will not be tolerated. Bring any problem to the show director at festival headquarters.
